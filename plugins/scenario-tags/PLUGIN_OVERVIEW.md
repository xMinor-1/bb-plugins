Turn the tasks your team repeats with agents into tags above the message box.

## What you get

- A thin row of tags above the message box, in thread and new-thread composers.
- **Scenarios** expands every scenario by group; a pin keeps a tag in the row.
- A tag opens a short form with its questions. Required questions, including
  "attach the file", must be answered before anything is inserted.
- The answers become a ready prompt in the message box. You read it and send it
  yourself.
- Make your own tags in a conversation: **New scenario** has the agent ask
  about the task, then shows you the tag and its form before saving. Turn a
  conversation that worked into a tag with **Save as scenario**. Your tags show
  under **Mine** with Change, Delete and Suggest to everyone.

## How it works

One setting holds the catalog: groups, tags, questions and a prompt template
for each. A build or an administrator writes it, so a new tag reaches everyone
at once, and each person can still pin their own. `bb scenario-tags check`
reports any entry that could not be read.

Nothing leaves the machine: the plugin only fills the message box.
