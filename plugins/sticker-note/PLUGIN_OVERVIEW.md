Sticker Note is a personal sticky-note board for your own small tasks, right inside bb.

Say a task out loud on the board, or type it, and it becomes a card with the date, time, importance and links filled in. "Tomorrow at 10 call the service", "On Thursday renew the insurance sravni.ru", "Someday buy vacuum filters" — each lands in the right column at once, with the understood parts highlighted.

Commands work the same way: "mark the parcel done", "move the service call to Wednesday at 12", "make the passport urgent", "delete the filters". When two cards match, the board asks which one. Every change has Undo.

The board has four columns by time. Today is ordered by the clock with a red "now" line, and overdue times turn red. Soon covers the next seven days, Later holds everything further out or without a date, Done keeps what you finished. Important cards carry a folded corner. Drag a card to reschedule it.

Open a card to edit it, or dictate into it: details go into its notes, and a request such as "ask the agent to compare the prices" starts a bb thread with the card as context.

A badge on the navigation item and a sticker button in the sidebar footer show how many tasks are left for today.

Dictation uses the voice service selected in Settings → AI services. The interface is in English and Russian.
